3. Shipping Guide

warning Name Matching Notice:
The sender's name on the parcel package MUST MATCH EXACTLY the applicant's name () to ensure fast verification.
1 Convenience Store (CU / GS25)
  • Pack your phone securely (use bubble wrap if possible).
  • Use the self-service Postbox terminal at any CU or GS25.
  • Select "Domestic Parcel" (국내택배).
  • Choose "Pay on Delivery / COD" (착불) - shipping is FREE for you.
  • Input the destination address below. Print waybill and scan at cashier.
2 Korea Post Office (우체국)
  • Go to any local Korea Post branch.
  • Ask for a "Cash on Delivery Parcel" (착불 소포) form.
  • Fill out the form with the receiver's address and phone number below.
  • Hand the parcel to the clerk. (No shipping payment required).
